Onboarding Funnel Rebuild

9 Payment Service Bank (9PSB) · Product Manager

The brief said the onboarding screens were the problem. Running the funnel myself in SQL and Power BI showed the drop was at identity verification, where a failed check returned no message and left the user with nothing to do next. We held the verification standard where it was and rebuilt the failure path instead: explicit failure reasons, a defined retry route, and instrumentation at each step. Verification failures fell 20% and monthly active users rose 40%, with the control untouched.
